watertile_d.dds / watertile_n.dds: the smooth water tile (diffuse and normal) the LOD fake water uses. Taken from CS Water Mod
(fxwatertileflat_d.dds / fxwatertileflat_n.dds), which is the same author's own mod, and shipped with AnvilLOD so the output does
not depend on any water mod being installed. Written to <output>\textures\anvillod\water\ when water stand-ins or water copies are made.
